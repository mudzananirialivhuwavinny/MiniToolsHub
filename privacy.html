Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Welcome to MiniTools Hub. We value your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be handled when you visit and use our website.

1. Information We Collect

MiniTools Hub currently provides simple online tools, such as calculators, text counters, timers, unit converters, and QR code generators.

We do not ask visitors to create an account to use the tools. Information you enter into a tool may be processed by that tool in your browser. Avoid entering passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information into any online tool.

Our website hosting provider or other services used by the website may process technical information, such as browser requests and server logs, as part of operating and protecting the website.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

MiniTools Hub does not intentionally require visitors to create accounts or enable cookies to use its basic tools. However, our hosting provider or future third-party services, such as advertising or analytics providers, may use cookies or similar technologies.

If we introduce services that use cookies, we will update this policy to explain them and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

3. Third-Party Services

Some website features may rely on third-party services. For example, a QR code feature may use an external library or service, depending on its implementation.

When you interact with a third-party service, that provider may process information under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the relevant provider's policies when applicable.

4. Advertising

MiniTools Hub may display third-party advertisements in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver advertisements, measure performance, or personalize ads where permitted.

If advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated to identify the relevant practices and explain available privacy choices.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with operating the website may be used to maintain site functionality, protect the site against misuse, understand technical problems, and improve the visitor experience.

6. Data Security

We aim to take reasonable steps to keep the website secure. However, no website or method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

MiniTools Hub is a general-purpose website and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through the website.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website features, services, or legal obligations change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page.

A direct contact email may be added when one is established.
